EnemySpriteFactory, Sumanth Gopal, Quality Review
This is a solid factory pattern, but the maintainability is a little weak because of the hard-coded sprite rectangles, direction
branches, and repeated timing logic. Each enemy method embeds raw sheets updates. The Goriya method specifically especially mixes
asset knowledge with direction logic in a way that is hard to extend safely.  A better approach would be to centralize sprite 
data into configuration objects or constants and reduce the conditional branching with a directional mapping strategy. 
This would make the class easier to read, update, and scale.
